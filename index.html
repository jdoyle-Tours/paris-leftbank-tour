The Left Bank

From the medieval gates of Contrescarpe to the gilded dome of the Académie Française — the walk that made French intellectual life.

The Route

The tour begins at Place de la Contrescarpe in the 5th arrondissement and follows a westward arc through the Latin Quarter, Saint-Germain-des-Prés, and the 6th, ending at the Académie Française on the quai de Conti.

Total distance: approximately 5.5km. Allow 5–7 hours with stops, reading, and café pauses. Every stop is independent — feel free to begin or end anywhere.

Recommended Pauses

  • After Stop 4 (Mosque): Mint tea and a pastry at the Café de la Mosquée — one of Paris's great hidden pleasures.
  • After Stop 8 (Luxembourg): Sit on one of the green iron chairs by the fountain for as long as you can.
  • After Stop 10 (Café de Flore): Walk next door to Les Deux Magots and order a café crème. You are sitting where Sartre and Hemingway sat.

Best Days

Tuesday through Saturday. The Musée de Cluny is closed Tuesdays. Most churches open daily but quieter on weekdays. The Luxembourg Gardens open every day at dawn.

Practical Notes

Wear your most comfortable shoes. The Left Bank is mostly flat but the stone streets are unforgiving over five hours.
